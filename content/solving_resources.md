## SudokuPad UserScripts
### Rules Resizer
A very handy script by Nell Gwen that allows you to adjust the location, size and font size of the rules and the on-screen numpad.  
[rules-resizer](files/rules-resizer.js)

### Extended Checks
Allows you to check the progress of an unfinished puzzle. It is similar to the built-in checkmark but offers additional checks.  
https://dev-sudokucolors.netlify.app/extendedchecks

### Sudokupad Colour Editor
Allows you to change colours of puzzles in SudokuPad. This includes line colours, but also other colours in the puzzle are available to edit.  
https://dev-sudokucolors.netlify.app/user-script/

### Sven Magic
Adds a button that resolves all singles in SudokuPad.  
https://github.com/yusitnikov/sudokupad-sven-magic
